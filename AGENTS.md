
## UI text visibility

- Whenever adding or changing UI text, check that the full text is visible, does not overlap adjacent text or controls, and remains accessible at different resolutions and window sizes.
- Measure wrapped prose and allocate its full height. Use scrollable content for long text; fit short fixed-size controls without ellipsis or truncation. Keep tree text fixed relative to its card when zooming or panning.
- Check at least 800×600, 1280×720, 1920×1080, and 3840×2160, plus minimum resizable-window dimensions and numeric/English guide variants. Distinguish compile/metric checks from actual visual checks in the report.
